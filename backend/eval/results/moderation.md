## Policy in use

- Listings: held for review when the rules flag them or the model sees medium or high risk (the best row above). The model alone misses many: it often names the right category but calls the risk low.
- Chat: with a model, the model decides, and it clears the rules' payment warning on honest messages; without one (the live demo), the rules warn.
- Reports: priority from the rules; the model adds what the content is and why.

Listing checks took 0.0 s each on average (laptop GPU; cached answers are instant on a rerun).
